

The guitar's strings snap;
a bow twangs, fires,
dry or wet. Something sharp
is tracing an arc, searching
a subject, a
twisted twisting mass. 
We, the quivering, the unquivering, 
we are the loosing, the
sheathing and unsheathing of 
teeth, bright 
against the dark.
Close your eyes tightly, 
keep out
the sunlight, 
convince me you mean it.

Every unordered set, every 
shouted word, 
ravels cleanly around the conductor's
baton; 
snake around scepter.
Breaking ground! (Whether
or not we are Romans, Neptune
has something to tell us)
Without ire -- with tired, patient,
persistent strokes -- the sea
eats the shore.
