
A train horn,
a leaping
stag, 
antlers pointed
toward the light.
Pointed 
in all directions.
Not a tree but 
a dandelion.
Blooming, blowing,
gone soon.
Drawing, like a monsoon in
the dry season,
every straying glance.

A half-hearted dance
becoming more hearted.
I'm starting from scratch
(or trying).
